# Demonstration Travel and Expense Policy, version v2.0

**SYNTHETIC RESEARCH DOCUMENT. NOT AN OFFICIAL AARHUS UNIVERSITY POLICY. All rules, amounts and dates are fictional.**

Published: 2025-08-01. In force from: 2025-09-01. Replaces: v1.0.
Issued by: Demonstration Finance Office (fictional).

## GEN-101 Scope

This policy applies to employees on official trips in Denmark and abroad. Students are not covered, except when they travel on behalf of their department with written approval from the head of department before the trip.

## TR-201 Taxis

Taxis may only be used with prior approval from the budget holder when public transport is available for the journey. When no reasonable public transport is available, no approval is needed, but the settlement must explain why a taxi was necessary. A receipt is always required.

## AC-301 Hotels in Denmark

Hotel costs in Denmark are reimbursed up to DKK 1,350 per night, including breakfast.

## EX-401 Receipts

Receipts are required for every expense, whatever the amount.

## ME-501 Meals without daily allowance

When no daily allowance is paid, documented meal costs are reimbursed up to DKK 350 per day.

## CC-601 Payment card

Employees should pay with the university payment card where possible. Expenses paid with a personal card are reimbursed through the settlement.

## ST-701 Settlement deadline

The settlement must be submitted within 60 days after the trip ends.
