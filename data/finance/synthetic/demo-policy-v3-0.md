# Demonstration Travel and Expense Policy, version v3.0

**SYNTHETIC RESEARCH DOCUMENT. NOT AN OFFICIAL AARHUS UNIVERSITY POLICY. All rules, amounts and dates are fictional.**

Published: 2026-02-10. In force from: 2026-03-01. Replaces: v2.1.
Issued by: Demonstration Finance Office (fictional).

## GEN-101 Scope

This policy applies to employees on official trips in Denmark and abroad. Students are not covered, except when they travel on behalf of their department with written approval from the head of department before the trip.

## TR-201 Taxis

Taxis generally require prior approval from the budget holder when public transport is available for the journey. Exception: no prior approval is needed when public transport is considered unsafe, or for journeys between 23:00 and 06:00. The reason must be written in the settlement. A receipt is always required.

## AC-301 Hotels in Denmark

Hotel costs in Denmark are reimbursed up to DKK 1,350 per night, including breakfast. A more expensive hotel requires approval from the head of department before booking.

## EX-401 Receipts

Receipts are required for every expense. If a receipt is lost, the employee may submit a signed declaration describing the expense instead, at most twice per calendar year.

## ME-501 Meals without daily allowance

When no daily allowance is paid, documented meal costs are reimbursed up to DKK 350 per day. Meals provided free of charge (for example at a conference) are deducted: breakfast 15%, lunch 30% and dinner 30% of the daily cap.

## CC-601 Payment card

Employees should pay with the university payment card where possible. Expenses paid with a personal card are reimbursed through the settlement.

## ST-701 Settlement deadline

The settlement must be submitted within 30 days after the trip ends. Later settlements require approval from the budget holder.
