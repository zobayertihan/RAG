# Demonstration Travel and Expense Policy, version v1.0

**SYNTHETIC RESEARCH DOCUMENT. NOT AN OFFICIAL AARHUS UNIVERSITY POLICY. All rules, amounts and dates are fictional.**

Published: 2025-03-14. In force from: 2025-04-01. Replaces: none.
Issued by: Demonstration Finance Office (fictional).

## GEN-101 Scope

This policy applies to employees on official trips in Denmark and abroad. Students are not covered, except when they travel on behalf of their department with written approval from the head of department before the trip.

## TR-201 Taxis

Taxi fares on official trips are reimbursed when the trip is work-related and a receipt is submitted with the settlement.

## AC-301 Hotels in Denmark

Hotel costs in Denmark are reimbursed up to DKK 1,200 per night, including breakfast.

## EX-401 Receipts

Receipts are required for every expense above DKK 100. Expenses of DKK 100 or less can be claimed without a receipt if they are listed in the settlement.

## ME-501 Meals without daily allowance

When no daily allowance is paid, documented meal costs are reimbursed up to DKK 300 per day.

## CC-601 Payment card

Employees should pay with the university payment card where possible. Expenses paid with a personal card are reimbursed through the settlement.

## ST-701 Settlement deadline

The settlement must be submitted within 60 days after the trip ends.
